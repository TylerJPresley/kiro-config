# Retro

Intent: turn what a session revealed into a concrete improvement to the steering, skills, agents or scripts, so the setup gets sharper with use.

## When to use
- A session or a feature closes.
- A repeated friction, a wasted effort, or a correction the owner made keeps coming up.
- A pattern worth keeping shows up and belongs in a durable place.

## How to work
1. Look back over the session for what helped, what cost time, and where a rule or a tool was missing.
2. Separate a one-off from a pattern: record a pattern the second time it appears with the `lessons` script, and bump it on each recurrence.
3. At the third sighting, propose promoting it to the cheapest durable form, in this order: a script, then a skill, then a steering line.
4. Write each proposal as a concrete change to a named file, with the reason and the evidence, and leave it in the proposals area for the owner.
5. Keep the proposal small and specific, so the owner can accept or veto it in one read.
