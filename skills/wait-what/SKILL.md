# Wait, what

Intent: restate a single message so a reader understands it on one read, with the meaning intact.

## When to use
- The owner asks what a message or a passage meant.
- A reply came out dense, full of jargon, or hard to follow.
- A term or an abbreviation in the message may be unfamiliar.

## How to work
1. Find the one claim or request the message carries, and lead with it in a plain sentence.
2. Replace jargon, abbreviations and internal labels with everyday words; define a term the reader may not know the first time it appears.
3. Break a long or tangled sentence into short ones, one idea each.
4. Keep every fact from the original; shorten by cutting filler, not meaning.
5. Check the result: could someone arriving with no context follow it on one read?
