# Framing

Intent: every rule, spec and document states direction by what we want and states limits plainly, so the reader knows the intended state and its boundaries.
Applies: when writing any rule, spec, plan or document an agent or a person reads.

- Direction is framed by what we want and why it works. A choice is never justified by listing what is bad.
- A problem is stated as the gap between what exists and what is intended, and the fix moves toward the intended state.
- A boundary is stated plainly as a limit: never, must not, only. A limit that is softened stops being a limit.
- Specs, plans and documents follow this: direction is affirmative and limits are explicit.
