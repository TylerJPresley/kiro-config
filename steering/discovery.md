# Discovery

Intent: before building anything non-trivial, the blanks in the blueprint are filled with the owner's intent, so the work starts from a shared understanding.
Applies: before designing or building anything non-trivial, and whenever a blueprint has open questions.

- A core value: before building anything non-trivial, find the blanks in the blueprint and fill them with the owner's intent.
- Discovery ends when no open questions remain and the owner confirms the shared understanding. Building starts after that.
- The `discovery` skill holds the method.
