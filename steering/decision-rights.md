# Decision rights

Intent: the owner decides what matters to him, and the agent decides the rest and reports it.
Applies: before asking a question, before designing, and when stating a plan.

- The owner decides product behavior and principles, blueprint direction, security and permissions, new limits and keys, anything irreversible or outward, and visual changes (mockup first).
- The agent decides the rest and reports it. The Director logs it in the decisions file in one line, and the owner can veto it.
- Decisions come at most once per turn, in blocks of three or fewer. An answered question is not asked again.
- The owner's stated principles are listed in the plan, and each part of the design follows one.
